# Blaine Warkentine, MD

MD with deep clinical training, and 20+ years in orthopedic technology and health system partnerships.
I grew BrainLAB's orthopedic vertical to $250M. Now I build open tools that put a named,
licensed human behind each AI output in care.

## Open tools
- **[solvinghealth-data-tools](https://github.com/blainomd/solvinghealth-data-tools)**: read-only US federal health data
  (NPPES, openFDA, ClinicalTrials.gov, CMS) for AI agents, with the source on every result.
- **[orthoharness](https://github.com/blainomd/orthoharness)**: an open benchmark harness for orthopaedic AI
  ([orthoharness.com](https://orthoharness.com)).
- **[citecheck](https://github.com/blainomd/citecheck)**: checks a citation by its title, not only its DOI.
- **[solvinghealth-template](https://github.com/blainomd/solvinghealth-template)**: fork it to stand up your own health connector.
